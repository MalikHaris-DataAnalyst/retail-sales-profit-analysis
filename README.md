# Retail Sales and Profit Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed retail sales and profit data using Python, Google Colab, and Microsoft Power BI to identify business performance trends and areas for improvement.

---

## 🛠 Tools Used

### Core Environment & Reporting
* **Python** — Data analysis and exploration
* **Google Colab** — Running Python code
* **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboards and visualizations
* **GitHub** — Version control and hosting

### Libraries
* **Pandas** — Data manipulation
* **Matplotlib** — Static data visualizations
* **Plotly** — Interactive charts

---

## ❓ Business Questions
The business needs to understand which product categories and sub-categories generate the most profit, where losses occur, and how discount levels and regional performance relate to profitability.

* How much sales and profit did the business generate?
* Which product categories generated the most profit?
* Which product subcategories were losing money?
* How were discounts associated with profitability?

---

## 📊 Key Findings
* **Total Sales:** Approximately **\$2.30 million**.
* **Total Profit:** Approximately **\$286,397**.
* **Top Category:** Technology generated the highest profit among the three product categories.
* **Loss-Making Areas:** Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies had combined losses of approximately **\$22,387**.
* **Discount Impact:** Higher discount levels were closely associated with losses in several parts of this dataset.

---

## 💡 Recommendations & Next Steps
1. **Investigate loss-making product sub-categories** to determine structural cost or supply issues.
2. **Review discount policies** and evaluate their direct impact on product profitability thresholds.
3. **Compare regional sales and profit performance** to identify geographical improvement opportunities.

## ⚠️ Limitations
* The dataset does not include order dates or order IDs, so this project does not analyze sales trends over time or unique order counts. 
* The findings describe patterns in this dataset and do not establish that discounts alone caused losses.
